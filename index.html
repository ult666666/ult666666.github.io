Frontier AI Valuation Lab

A valuation and financing studio for frontier AI labs. You set how much money a lab needs to raise and how much of the company its owners want to keep, then test financing plans against bear, base and bull cases. A separate Python script recomputes every number the browser shows, so a bug in either one turns up as a disagreement.

About

Most of my projects handle money or data, and I test them against answers I didn’t write. quantkit has to reproduce Hull’s worked example, a call at 4.76 and a put at 0.81. powerlift has to match Jennison and Turnbull’s published tables, and its own simulation shows why that care matters: peek at an A/A test ten times, stop at the first p below 0.05, and 19.5% of them come out “significant” against a 5% target.

I write mostly Python, TypeScript and SQL. The tools I like best do one job well and say plainly what they don’t do.

If you’d rather read than click around, start with how the valuation math is checked, or with what LineageGuard refuses to approve.
