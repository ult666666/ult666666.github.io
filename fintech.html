My role

What I did on this project

I built Frontier AI Valuation Lab as an independent, AI-assisted portfolio project. The work connects financial-model review, product and business framing, quantitative method design, full stack implementation and reproducible verification.

Capital Strategy Studio compares submitted hypothetical plans under explicit objectives and named scenarios. It does not infer funding need from economic FCFF, assign probabilities to cases or model investment-driven operating benefits. The selected metric allocates a signed modeled equity residual under equal rights; it is not an investment return or a limited-liability share price.

The financial model uses illustrative assumptions and incomplete public disclosures. The primary-round lens retains proceeds as cash and excludes fees, preference waterfalls, option-pool changes, secondary sales and future rounds. The method and verification record make these boundaries inspectable.
