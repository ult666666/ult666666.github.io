04 / MY ROLE

Financial research.
Product decisions.
Shipped software.

I built Frontier AI Valuation Lab as an independent, AI-assisted portfolio project. My work connects financial-model review, interaction design, full stack implementation and reproducible verification.

The project presents conditional operating enterprise values from illustrative assumptions and incomplete public disclosures. Value Horizon is a truncation lens: it preserves the forecast through 2036, includes the selected terminal years, and assigns no cash flows or residual value after the cutoff. It does not forecast a company's closure or an investor's holding-period return.

Financing Lens uses separate hypothetical balances and transaction terms. Its simple primary round assumes equal economic rights and retained cash, with no fees, option-pool changes, SAFEs, secondary sales or preference waterfall. The example does not establish either company's balance sheet or cap table.

Application 3.5.0 · Financial engine 3.0.0 · Research snapshot 18 Oct 2026. No live financial-data refresh, security-specific price target or investment-return claim.
