Follow the implementation

Follow the implementation
Follow the exact McNemar test, seeded paired bootstrap intervals and minimum detectable effects into the --gate options. On synthetic 100-case suites where 10% of cases flip at random, any-regression failed 99.4% of unchanged suites and McNemar 1.1%.
Project scope

powerlift

Experiment statistics with power analysis, multi-arm (A/B/n) tests with Dunnett’s correction, CUPED, sequential monitoring and Bayesian tools.

  • Python
  • SciPy
  • statsmodels
A scenario to explore
Read a seeded synthetic CSV with one control and three variants: the SRM check runs across all four arms, and Dunnett’s test finds only variant D different while holding the family-wise false-win rate at 5%. Plan the sample size for three arms, or compare raw and CUPED-adjusted estimates in the two-arm readout.
Follow the implementation
Follow the many-to-one analysis into the max-|t| distribution, where the shared control turns a k-dimensional integral into a two-dimensional one. Results agree with scipy.stats.dunnett, and simulated family-wise error under the global null is 5.1% for Dunnett against 13.5% unadjusted with three variants.
Project scope
Statistical results depend on study design and data assumptions; a significant result alone is not a business decision. Multi-arm comparisons are many-to-one and single-step, and they pool the arms’ variance.

searchlite

A full-text search engine with compressed postings, MaxScore top-k pruning, explainable ranking and retrieval evaluation.

  • TypeScript
  • BM25F
A scenario to explore
Compare two saved local indexes with your own relevance judgments in the browser, inspect query-level regressions and download the native-compatible report. Then run the pruning property tests: on random corpora, queries, filters and limits, the pruned top-k equals scoring every match.
Follow the implementation
Follow delta and varint blocks of 128 documents with skip entries and per-block score bounds into MaxScore windows. At 10,000 synthetic documents, median top-10 latency fell from 2.06 to 0.30 ms (glossary) and 8.48 to 0.57 ms (Zipf) with byte-identical rankings. The local-file worker shares validation, ranking and the strict per-query gate with the native command.
Project scope
Small in-memory library. Pruning gains are smaller at 1,000 documents, one phrase-heavy query is slower, and block-max WAND is not implemented. Files stay in page memory, and exported reports contain query text and document IDs. Synthetic judgments and timing fixtures do not establish general retrieval quality or production latency.
